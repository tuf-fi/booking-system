About the inn

Lantaw Inn has five room types across three floors, from a budget single for solo travelers to a family room that sleeps six. The owners live on the ground floor, so there's always someone around to help with directions, tricycle rides or a late check-in.

The best spot in the house is the roof deck. On clear mornings Mayon Volcano fills the view, and most guests end up having their coffee up there. In the evening it's a quiet place to sit after a day at Cagsawa or the Embarcadero.

We're on a quiet side street, a short ride from the city center, SM City Legazpi and the bay. The exact address is sent with your booking confirmation.

Questions guests ask

Is breakfast included?

No, but you can order a Filipino breakfast (longganisa, tapa or tocino with rice, egg and coffee) for ₱180 the night before. You're also free to cook in the shared kitchen.

Can I check in early or check out late?

If your room is ready, early check-in is free. Late check-out until 2 PM costs ₱300 if nobody is checking in after you. Message us on the day.

Do you accept walk-in guests?

Yes, if a room is free. Booking online is safer, especially from December to May and during the Ibalong Festival in August.

Can I see Mayon from my room?

From the Deluxe double and the Family room, yes. From every room you can walk up to the roof deck. Mayon is clearest early in the morning.

How do I pay the balance?

Pay the rest at check-in, in cash or by GCash.
